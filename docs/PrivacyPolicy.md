# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Clipboard Manager is a macOS application that stores clipboard history on your device.

## What the app accesses

- Clipboard content that you copy on your Mac, including text and images

## How data is used

- Clipboard data is used only to display your local clipboard history inside the app
- Clipboard data is not uploaded to any server
- Clipboard data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking

## Data retention

- Clipboard history stays on your device
- You can delete individual items or clear all unpinned items at any time
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

## Third parties

- Clipboard Manager does not use third-party advertising SDKs
- Clipboard Manager does not use analytics or remote tracking services
