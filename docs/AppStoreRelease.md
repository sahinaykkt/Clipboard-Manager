## Description

Clipboard Manager keeps a local history of the text and images you copy on your Mac.

Access it from the menu bar icon. Search older clips, pin important items, and copy anything back with a double-click.

### Highlights

- Stores copied text and images automatically
- Lives in the menu bar for quick access
- Search and filter your clipboard history
- Pin important items so they stay available
- All clipboard history stored on device — nothing leaves your Mac

## Review Notes

Clipboard Manager is a menu bar macOS utility that monitors the system pasteboard to build a local clipboard history.

Clipboard contents are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to any server.
