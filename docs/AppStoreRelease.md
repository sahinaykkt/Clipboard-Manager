## Description

Clipboard Manager keeps a local history of the text and images you copy on your Mac.

Access it from the menu bar icon. Search older clips, pin important items, copy anything back with a single click, and manage startup and history limits from Preferences.

### Highlights

- Stores copied text and images automatically
- Lives in the menu bar for quick access
- Keeps clipboard history after app relaunches and Mac restarts
- Search and filter your clipboard history
- Pin important items so they stay available
- Copy any saved item with a single click
- Launch at login and history limits can be configured in Preferences
- All clipboard history stored on device — nothing leaves your Mac

## Review Notes

Clipboard Manager is a menu bar macOS utility that monitors the system pasteboard to build a local clipboard history.

Clipboard contents are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to any server.

Version 1.1 adds persistent local history, a Preferences window for startup and retention settings, and single-click item restore from the menu bar list.
